When Roundhouse serves you tools, under the name `roundhouse`, they're for pull requests and for the comments the person leaves on the workspace's diff, and their own instructions say which pull requests they cover. Before you reach for `gh pr view` or `gh pr checks` to see where one of those stands, such as whether its checks have passed, whether it's in the merge queue or whether it has merged, look among them for one that answers it. `gh` is still the way to open, change, queue or merge a pull request, to read what's written on it, such as its reviews, and to read a run's logs. When the person mentions their comments on the diff, read them with those tools.
