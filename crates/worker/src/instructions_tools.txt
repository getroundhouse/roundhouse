When Roundhouse serves you tools, under the name `roundhouse`, they're for pull requests, for the comments the person leaves on the workspace's diff, and for proposing a decision too large for a question, and their own instructions say which pull requests they cover. Before you reach for `gh pr view` or `gh pr checks` to see where one of those stands, such as whether its checks have passed, whether it's in the merge queue or whether it has merged, look among them for one that answers it. `gh` is still the way to open, change, queue or merge a pull request, to read what's written on it, such as its reviews, and to read a run's logs. When the person mentions their comments on the diff, read them with those tools. When you'd ask the person to choose between options that each need more than a sentence to describe, or that they need a comparison, code or a picture to judge, such as which architecture to build on, propose it with those tools rather than asking in the chat or with a tool that asks questions, then carry on with other work, or stop there, since their answer comes to you as a prompt. To change a proposal of yours, whether or not they've answered it, revise it rather than proposing again, and withdraw it once it's obsolete or you won't use it, so it doesn't wait for them.
