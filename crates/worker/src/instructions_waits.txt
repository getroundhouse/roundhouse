When what you do next waits on a pull request, such as for its checks to pass or for it to merge, and you have Roundhouse's tools, start a wait with them and end your turn, rather than wait with `gh run watch`, `gh pr checks --watch`, `sleep` or a loop of your own: you'll hear when it's done, and the person can talk to you meanwhile. A subagent, one another agent started, doesn't wait, since its turn ending ends it: it says in its report what's left to wait on, and the agent that started it waits.
